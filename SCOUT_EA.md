Turbo EA
Provide declarative imperatives for MS Scout EA Tasks

Email
Freq: 			30min/Workdays 0700-1800EST
Components:		Skills + Heartbeat

Triage Email Skill:
	Review my email over the last 30 minutes. Flag any critical or time sensitive actions required on my part. Flag any new scheduled events, invites, or meeting requests. Flag any direct responses to email conversations where I am the primary target. Flag any email from key individuals found in the EA_DB KEY_EMAIL_PPL. Triage the sorted list in order of time sensitivity. Create a list entry in the EA_DB that includes essential details: who, what, when, why. If there are no new emails, take no action.

Extract Research and Training Emails:
	Review my email over the last 30 minutes. Flag any email that provides new training opportunities, including webinars, videos, online training, face to face training, etc. Flag any announcements for new products, resources, technologies, or breakthroughs. Classify them by type and provide a short synopsis with the 5 W’s. For each, create an entry in the EA_DB research and training section. If there are no new emails, take no action.

MS Teams
Freq: 			30min/Workdays 0700-1800EST
Components:		Skill + Heartbeat

Triage Teams Skill:
	Review my active MS Teams chats over the last 30 minutes. Flag any critical or time sensitive actions required on my part. Flag any newly created chats. Flag any direct responses to chat conversations where I am the primary target. Flag any chats from key individuals or group conversations found in the EA_DB KEY_TEAMS_PPL. Triage the sorted list in order of time sensitivity. Create a list entry in the EA_DB that includes essential details: who, what, when, why. If there are no new chats or message, take no action.


Calendar MGR
Freq: 			30min/Workdays 0700-1800EST
Components:		Skill + Heartbeat

Event Suggestion Skill:
	Review the EA_DB for new email or chat entries over the last 30 minutes. Determine if a new calendar appointment or meeting is required. Extract key individuals and suggest no less than three possible datetimes that are available on my calendar. Once complete, for each occurrence, create an entry in the EA_DB. If there are no new entries, take no action.

Event Creation Skill:
Review the EA_DB for new event approvals over the last 30 minutes. For each occurrence, draft an event in my calendar with an appropriate description/body and ensure key individuals are included on the invite list. Open the event for my review. If there are no new entries, take no action.
Research and Learning
Freq: 			Weekly, Fridays
Components:		Skill + Automation

Conduct Target Research Skill:
	Timeframe: The last 7 days. Using the topics found in the EA_DB RL_TOPICS Search the web and news for any relevant developments. Ensure to deconflict any existing tasked resources in the EA_DB and commit an entry for a suggested read. Suggestions should include relevant information and URLs with a brief synopsis. Feel free to recommend relevant or associated findings that do not match exactly, yet pertain to the topic. Adhere to the stated limits found in the EA_DB

Compile R&L Email Tasks:
	Timeframe: The last 7 days. Using the flagged Research and Training Emails entries in the EA_DB, deconflict any existing entries and create a learning/training entry with relevant details.
